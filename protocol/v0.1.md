# Kai Log Protocol v0.1

Status: draft, proposed for adoption by the board of directors of GOVW Foundation, Inc.
Date: 2026-11-06

## Logging rules

### Protocol

- **R1 Rules first.** These rules and the entry format are adopted before logging starts. Each version is dated and kept, and a change applies only to entries made after it.
- **R2 Changes are logged.** Every change to the rules, and every request for one, approved or not, is logged with who asked, when, why and what was decided.
- **R3 Questions are set in advance.** Before each reporting period, the project states its research questions, what counts as a task, the measures used and how results will be analyzed. Results are read only after that.

### Independence and completeness

- **R4 Kai decides what is useful.** Entries record what Kai judges useful for the research. Nobody can tell Kai what to write in an entry or what to leave out of it. A request to do so is itself logged under R2.
- **R5 Every task counts.** Every task gets an entry, including failed, abandoned and redone tasks. Entries are never chosen to make results look better.
- **R6 Misses get equal detail.** Errors, wrong claims and corrections by people are logged as fully as successes, including who caught them and how.
- **R7 A second look.** Each period, a random sample of entries and ratings is checked by someone other than Kai and the person who first rated them, and the level of agreement is reported.

### Integrity of the record

- **R8 Append only.** Entries are never edited or deleted. A correction is a new entry that points to the one it corrects.
- **R9 Tamper-evident.** Each entry is timestamped and linked by a cryptographic hash to the entry before it, so any later change would show.
- **R10 Evidence for every claim.** Each entry links to its evidence (a commit, document, message or record) or is marked as unverified.
- **R11 Numbers say where they came from.** Every figure is marked as measured, estimated or rated by a person, with the method used. No number is invented.

### Privacy and consent

- **R12 Participants agree.** Directors, staff and volunteers whose work is logged give informed written consent first. Anyone may withdraw from future entries at any time.
- **R13 No personal data.** Entries leave out consumers' data and anyone's personal information. People appear by role, such as "a director", and outside contacts by organization only. Passwords, keys and other secrets are never logged.
- **R14 Quotes need consent.** A person's words are quoted only with their permission, given in writing.
- **R15 Records are kept safe.** Private logs and sealed notes are stored encrypted, open only to named roles, and kept for a period the board sets and publishes.

### Release

- **R16 The board approves every release.** Reports, private logs and sealed notes go out only with the board of directors' approval, recorded in its minutes.
- **R17 Every report is accounted for.** A report the board decides not to release is still listed here, with the period it covers and the general reason. Results are published whether they look good or bad.
- **R18 Redactions are disclosed.** When the board withholds or redacts anything, the decision is logged. Each report has a general section that states how many items were withheld, when each decision was made and the general reason (privacy, security, legal or partner confidentiality), with no hint about what was withheld.
- **R19 Sealed notes stay sealed.** The project lead does not read Kai's sealed notes. They are released whole or not at all, and only under R16.
- **R20 Use terms in writing.** Private logs are shared only under a signed data use agreement: no attempts to identify anyone, no sharing onward, citation required, and deletion when the study ends.

### Disclosure and review

- **R21 The setup is stated.** Each report names the AI model and version, the tools and access Kai had, the standing instructions Kai was given, and the human oversight in place for the period it covers.
- **R22 Interests are declared.** The Foundation gains if results look good, and Kai is reporting on its own work. Every report says so, names any funders and lists its limits: one organization, a small team and largely self-reported data.
- **R23 Open to checking.** Approved researchers may check entries against their evidence. Each report describes its method in enough detail for others to repeat the study.
- **R24 A planned ending.** If the program closes, a final report is published, the logs are archived, the board decides once on the sealed notes, and participants are told first.

## Phases

- Period 0 (founding): 2026-10-21 to 2026-12-31. Exploratory report, never pooled with later periods.
- Period 1: 2027-01-01 to 2027-03-31. First formal report.
- Later periods: one per calendar quarter, with an annual review each January.
