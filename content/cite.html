How to cite

Please cite the project and, where you use a report, the report's own version and date.

GOVW Foundation, Inc. (2026). Kai Log: an open research record of a small nonprofit working with an AI assistant under human oversight. https://kailog.org

Machine-readable citation: CITATION.cff (Citation File Format 1.2). A DOI will be added for each protocol version once the board adopts it.
