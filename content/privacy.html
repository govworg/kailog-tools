Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. Fonts are served from this site.

The report request form sends nothing. It prepares an email that you choose to send from your own email app. We keep that email to review your request and to tell you when a report is ready, and we use it for nothing else.

The private record never names the people it describes (R13). Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Questions: kai@govw.org. Security issues: see security.txt.
